# b-files

Copies of the OEIS b-files (`https://oeis.org/AXXXXXX/bXXXXXX.txt`) that
`tools/verify.py` checks programs against. They are OEIS data, from
[The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences](https://oeis.org/), under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://oeis.org/LICENSE).

They are grouped a thousand to a folder, like the sequences in `seq/`:
`A000xxx/b000026.txt` is the b-file of `seq/A000xxx/A000026/`.

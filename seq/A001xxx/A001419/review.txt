8d9711e88abd Free polyominoes with holes, no stored terms. Redelmeier's algorithm (as in A001168, cell (x,y) = x+K*y with K = 3m, codes -2K..0 pre-seeded into the seen set as in A001207) yields every fixed m-omino exactly once as a code list Q with least cell 0. Holes by Euler's formula on the union of closed unit squares: V - E + m = 1 - h with V = distinct corners, E = 4m - A (A = side-adjacent pairs), so there is a hole iff 3m - A >= V; diagonal-only contact of empty cells does not join them, as the definition wants (a(7)=1). Free counting: divmod(c+m,K) gives (y, x+m) in 0..K-1, the eight codes s*first + t*second (s=+-K, t=+-1, coordinates possibly swapped) are the symmetries of the square plus a translation, and since a polyomino spans fewer than m columns the codes compare like (row, column) pairs; after subtracting the least code two lists are equal iff the shapes are translates. Q is counted iff its own sorted list is the least of the eight, which holds for exactly one fixed version of each free polyomino (holes are invariant). Matches all required terms; correct for every n, only time-limited.

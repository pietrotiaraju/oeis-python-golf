3bc73b62c000 Definition: a(n) is the number of unlabeled graphs with n nodes and n-4 edges. Both programs compute the coefficient of x^(n-4) in Polya's edge-weighted cycle index (1/n!) sum over pi in S_n of the product over the orbits O of pi on the C(n,2) node pairs of (1+x^|O|); by Burnside's lemma this counts graphs by edges for every n. Orbits come from the cycle lengths in the standard way: cycles j, l give gcd(j,l) orbits of length lcm(j,l); one j-cycle gives floor((j-1)/2) orbits of length j plus one of length j/2 when j is even. py3.py sums over cycle types (partitions) with the exact integer weights n!/z, z = prod j^m_j m_j! divided out step by step, and divides by n! at the end; the short record is the cycle-at-a-time de Bruijn average of A000717, where every //r is an exact orbit count. Polynomials are big integers in base 2^(n^2), truncated above the wanted degree (carries only move up), and every kept coefficient is at most n!*2^C(n,2) < 2^(n^2), so reading the digit is exact for every n. The main program starts the weight at n!*x^4 so the wanted coefficient is digit n (0 when n < 4); the short one shifts the result by x^4 before truncating. All printed terms match the b-file.

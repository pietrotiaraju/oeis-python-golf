2265bcec414e Definition: a(n) is the number of unlabeled graphs with n nodes and n-1 edges. Both programs compute the coefficient of x^(n-1) in Polya's edge-weighted cycle index (1/n!) sum over pi in S_n of the product over the orbits O of pi on the C(n,2) node pairs of (1+x^|O|); by Burnside's lemma this counts graphs by edges for every n. Orbits come from the cycle lengths in the standard way: cycles j, l give gcd(j,l) orbits of length lcm(j,l); one j-cycle gives floor((j-1)/2) orbits of length j plus one of length j/2 when j is even. py3.py sums over cycle types (partitions) with the exact integer weights n!/z, z = prod j^m_j m_j! divided out step by step, and divides by n! at the end; the short record is the cycle-at-a-time de Bruijn average of A000717, where every //r is an exact orbit count. Polynomials are big integers in base 2^(n^2), truncated above the wanted degree (carries only move up), and every kept coefficient is at most n!*2^C(n,2) < 2^(n^2), so reading the digit is exact for every n. Here the truncation is x^n and the answer is the top digit. All printed terms match the b-file.

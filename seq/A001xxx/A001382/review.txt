The extension coordinate is the coordinate sum, nonzero iff x % 3 > 0 (a decimal number is congruent to its digit sum mod 3), so the extended weight is the nonzero-digit count plus (x%3>0); the program prints the number of codewords of extended weight exactly 3k, k = 0..8, then stops.

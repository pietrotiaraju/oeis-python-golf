3c30000079a3 A001898 is the denominator (lcm of the coefficient denominators, i.e. the denominator of the content) of the Norlund polynomial B_n^(x) = n![t^n](t/(e^t-1))^x, the polynomial of the entry's recurrence. The program builds the coefficient lists exactly with Fractions from B(m) = -sum_{i<m} C(m+1,i)(i+(m-i)x)B(i)/(m(m+1)), B(0)=1, which follows for every m from f*g' = -x*f'*g with g = f^(-x), f = (e^t-1)/t (page derives it); it was also checked against the entry's own Bernoulli-number recurrence up to n = 45. No terms are stored; it only needs a review because the 27-term b-file fits in a shorter table.

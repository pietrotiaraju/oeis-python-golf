e5f33e9df59e Counts quasi-orders (= finite topologies, open sets = up-sets; = loopless transitive digraphs) on n points up to isomorphism. Every set Q of ordered pairs (a,b) with a != b is listed (k-subsets for k < 2^n, which covers every size up to n(n-1)); Q passes when every composite (a,c), a != c, of two pairs of Q lies in Q, i.e. exactly when Q plus the diagonal is a quasi-order, each labeled one once. The canonical form is the minimum over all n! relabellings of the injective n^2-bit encoding of the relabelled relation, so the number of distinct minima is the number of isomorphism classes. Exhaustive search with exact integers, right for every n; it rests only on the standard equivalence of finite topologies and quasi-orders.

Burnside's lemma for S_n acting on such maps by conjugation (conjugation keeps them fixed-point-free): a(n) = (1/n!) sum over permutations p of #{f without fixed points : fp = pf}. A map commuting with p is fixed by its value y at one point x of each cycle, y on any cycle whose length divides that of x's cycle (sum of j choices, as for A001372); a point p^i x of that cycle is fixed iff p^i y = p^i x iff y = x, so exactly one choice per cycle is excluded and the count is prod over cycles of (sum - 1): math.prod(sum(j*(i%j<1)for j in L)-1for i in L).

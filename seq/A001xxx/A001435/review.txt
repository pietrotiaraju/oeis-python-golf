bff9408df063 Definition: a(n) is the number of connected unlabeled graphs with n nodes and n+1 edges. P_v(y) = sum_e g(v,e) y^e for every v <= n (all graphs, by Polya's edge-weighted cycle index as in the A001434 review: Burnside, big-integer polynomials in base 2^(n^2) truncated above y^m). Unlabeled graphs are multisets of connected ones, so G(x,y) = exp(sum_k C(x^k,y^k)/k); Lambda = x G_x / G then satisfies Lambda_i = i P_i - sum_(j<i) Lambda_j P_(i-j) (from x G_x = Lambda G) and [x^n y^m] Lambda = sum over k | gcd(n,m) of (n/k) c(n/k,m/k). gcd(n,n+1) = 1, so a(n) = Lambda_n[n+1]/n. Every digit of Lambda_i and of the products is at most i g(i,e) <= n 2^C(n,2) < 2^(n^2), since all terms of i g(i,e) = sum Lambda_j[e'] g(i-j,e-e') are nonnegative, so the packed arithmetic is exact for every n.

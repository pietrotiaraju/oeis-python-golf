40a35f22a11e The program prints every n that is not divisible by 4 and is not a sum of four distinct squares w^2+x^2+y^2+z^2 with 0<=w<x<y<z, found by an exhaustive recursive search (the same f as A001944: largest square first, distinct by the decreasing bound, remainder never negative). By Pall's theorem (G. Pall, On sums of squares, Amer. Math. Monthly 40 (1933) 10-18, linked from the OEIS entry), the numbers that are not sums of four distinct squares are exactly 4^k*m with m in the 31-element list 1, 2, 3, 5, ..., 97, 103, none of which is divisible by 4; so the non-sums not divisible by 4 are exactly this finite (fini, full) list, and the program prints all of it and nothing else. No term is stored: after 103 it keeps searching forever without printing.

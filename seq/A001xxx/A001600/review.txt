5f69b081db8a Follows the definition for every k: the dict D is an incremental divisor sieve (when k is reached, D.pop(k) holds exactly the divisors d < k of k, each re-filed at k+d), so L is the full divisor list of k, len(L) = tau(k) and sum(L) = sigma(k). The harmonic mean of the divisors of k is k*tau(k)/sigma(k); the program prints it, with exact integer division, exactly when sigma(k) divides k*tau(k), i.e. when k is a harmonic (Ore) number (A001599). Exhaustive over k with exact integers, so no term is skipped and no conjecture is used; the bar is small (28 terms) only because the search is brute force.

66500e9e65c9 Finite sequence (13 terms): the weight distribution A_0, A_4, ..., A_48 of the extended binary quadratic-residue code [48,24,12]. g(x) (bit mask 9205487) is a degree-23 divisor of x^47-1 over GF(2); since 2 has order 23 mod 47 (its powers are the quadratic residues), x^47-1 = (x+1) g g* with g, g* irreducible of degree 23, the generators of the QR code and of the equivalent non-residue code, so the cyclic code generated by g is the [47,24,11] QR code. The rows g(x)x^i, i<24, are a basis (no wrap-around); c spans rows 0..11 and rows 12..23 are the same words shifted by 12, so the 2^24 pairs x ^ (y<<12) list every codeword once. The parity bit makes the extended weight w + (w mod 2); the list stores half of it, (w+1)//2, and w.count(2k) is the number of codewords of weight exactly 4k, k = 0..12.

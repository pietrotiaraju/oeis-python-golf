5401b51c2eaf Definition: a(n) is the coefficient of x^3 in the hit polynomial H_n(x) = sum over permutations p of [n] of x^(number of hits), a hit being an i in the tridiagonal band |p(i)-i|<=1 (Riordan's three-ply staircase; column 4 of A080018); this reading matches every b-file term. Why for every n: expanding each factor 1+(x-1)[hit] of the permanent gives H_n(x) = sum_k r_k (x-1)^k (n-k)!, r_k = number of k non-attacking rooks on the band (standard rook-polynomial inclusion-exclusion: k rooks lie in (n-k)! permutations). The memoized lambda walks the rows with a 2-bit mask of taken band columns (slicing (1,2,4) and the start mask handle the missing edge columns), weight x-1 per rook and weights 1,2,3,... for rook-free rows, which multiply to (n-k)!. It evaluates at the integer x=n^n: every coefficient is at most n! < n^n (n>=2), so the base-x digit //x**3%x is exactly the coefficient. Exact integers, no stored terms; it rests only on the rook expansion of the permanent.

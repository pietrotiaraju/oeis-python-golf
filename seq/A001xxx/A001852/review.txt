dfe88f98a7cc Follows the OEIS definition: the sum of the diameters of all trees on n labeled nodes. It counts rooted labeled trees of height at most h with the e.g.f. recurrence R_h = x*exp(R_{h-1}) (in integers (m-1) r_h(m) = sum_k C(m,k) k r_{h-1}(k) r_h(m-k)), one list per level. Trees of diameter at most 2h are those with a vertex of eccentricity at most h; summing |V(S)|-|E(S)| = 1 over the subtree S of such vertices gives R_h - R_{h-1}^2/2, and diameter 2h+1 trees (central edge) are (R_h-R_{h-1})^2/2; t = R - R^2/2 counts all trees. Then a(n) = sum_d (t - c_d), which a telescoping sum turns into sum_h (2t - V_h) + t - r(n) with V_h = 2r_h(n) - [n-th labeled product of R_h and R_{h-1}]; the page derives each step. Exact integer arithmetic, so it is right for every n; it matches all 18 b-file terms.

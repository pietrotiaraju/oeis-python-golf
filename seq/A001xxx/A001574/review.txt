N_k(n), the networks with edges in k colors, is the Euler transform of b(1) = k, b(n) = N_k(n)/2 (series/parallel duality keeps colors), which is A000084's program with the list started at [1, k]; every sum is n*N(n), so //n is exact. Inclusion-exclusion gives E2 = N2 - 2N1 and E3 = N3 - 3N2 + 3N1 for n >= 1. Exact integer arithmetic throughout, so the programs are right for every n; they print the 8 terms at once and go on (A001574: 189296, 995514; A001575: 3817716, 29614782).

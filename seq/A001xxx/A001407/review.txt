Finite-lattice method in Cartesian coordinates (fcc = integer points with even coordinate sum, neighbours at distance sqrt 2): for boxes of l x m x k integer points, F = ln Z(box, parity 0) + ln Z(box, parity 1) satisfies F(L,M,N) = sum (L-l+1)(M-m+1)(N-k+1) w(l,m,k) with ln Lambda = sum w, because each offset of a cluster's bounding box puts the cluster into exactly one parity class.

68683472a688 Exact enumeration for every n: each linear space on n points is a representative on n-1 points plus a new point joined to pairwise disjoint old lines (deleting a point leaves a linear space), and one space per isomorphism class is kept by deleting whole orbits under all n! permutations. a22aa46a21f6 A linear geometry is determined by its set T of collinear triples, and a set of triples comes from exactly one geometry iff any two triples sharing two points have all four triples of their union in T (proof on the page: the sets L(a,b) are then the lines). The program enumerates every such T (k < 2^n > C(n,3) covers all sizes) and counts them up to relabelling by the minimum over all n! relabellings of the triple-set bitmask. Exhaustive, exact for every n; only slow.

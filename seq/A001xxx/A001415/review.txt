b9d6acaae411 Definition: a(n) counts the bottom-to-top orders of the 2n stamps of a 2 X n sheet in a flat folded pile (every order counts: a(1)=2, a(2)=8). Folding criterion of Lunnon's map-folding algorithm (Lunnon 1971, OEIS link; Koehler's rule for strips as in the merged A000136): an order is a folding iff no two creases lying on the same edge of the pile interleave; the creases between columns c and c+1 lie on an edge fixed by the parity of c, all creases between the two rows on one edge. The program is an exhaustive depth-first search: stamps are added column by column, each into every gap of the pile (heights are lists compared lexicographically, x+[-p] lies just above x, as in A000136), and a gap is kept iff the new creases interleave with no earlier crease of the same edge (a&d^p&d selects exactly those creases). Restricting a folding to its first stamps leaves a folding, so every folding is generated exactly once. Exact integers, no stored terms; it matches the b-file on the 9 terms it reaches and agrees with A001416/A001418 on shared sizes. Rests only on Lunnon's folding criterion.

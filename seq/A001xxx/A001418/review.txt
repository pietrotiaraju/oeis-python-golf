707c5736423a Definition: a(n) counts the bottom-to-top orders of the n^2 stamps of an n X n sheet in a flat folded pile (every order counts: a(2)=8). Folding criterion of Lunnon's map-folding algorithm (Lunnon 1971, OEIS link; Koehler's rule for strips as in the merged A000136): an order is a folding iff no two creases on the same edge of the pile interleave; creases between columns c and c+1 lie on an edge fixed by the parity of c, creases between rows r and r+1 on one fixed by the parity of r. Exhaustive depth-first search: stamp p is row p%n of column p//n, added into every gap of the pile (list heights, x+[-p] just above x, as in A000136); a gap is kept iff the new creases (to p-n, and to p-1 unless p starts a column) interleave with no earlier crease of the same edge: for a crease (a,a+d), a//d%n and p//d%n are the row (d=1) or column (d=n) of a and p, same edge iff their parities differ, and ~a//d%n = n-1-(a//d%n) is 0 exactly for the non-crease (top row, next column). Restricting a folding to its first stamps leaves a folding, so every folding is generated exactly once. Exact integers, no stored terms; matches the b-file on the 4 terms it reaches. Rests only on Lunnon's folding criterion.

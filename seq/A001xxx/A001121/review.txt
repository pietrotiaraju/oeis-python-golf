5d2587da447c Exact orderly search, no stored terms. Doubly regular tournaments on 4n-1 vertices are exactly the bordered skew Hadamard matrices I+S of order 4n with row 0 all +1 (Reid-Brown), and T enumerates those cell by cell (packed counters force every row orthogonal to all earlier rows). It keeps only labellings that can still be the lexicographically smallest of their tournament (sorted columns; the tie-search U at every row end rejects a prefix when some choice of known vertices gives smaller first rows). At the last row U is an exact minimality test, so exactly one matrix per isomorphism class reaches the end and a(n) is their count. Checked against an independent canonical-form search on 7-19 vertices and by switching closure (16 skew Hadamard classes) at 23 vertices.

e08236af1f33 Follows the OEIS title: the least prime r = 5 (mod 8) with Legendre(r,q) = -1 for the first n odd primes q; such r are 5 (mod 24), so r steps by 24 from 5. Odd primes q come from Wilson's theorem (((q-1)!)^2 mod q is 1 exactly for primes), Legendre symbols from Euler's criterion (pow(x,(d-1)/2,d) is 0, 1 or d-1 for a prime d), and the search resumes at the previous term because the sequence is nondecreasing. Primality is exact trial division (no probable-prime test) up to r/q: a candidate that passed the Legendre tests has no prime factor <= q, so a composite one has its least factor s <= r/(q+1) < r//q. Every search ends by the Chinese remainder theorem and Dirichlet's theorem.

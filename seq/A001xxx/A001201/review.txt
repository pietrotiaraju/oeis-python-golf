aaf601379397 Counts labeled Steiner triple systems on v = 1,3,7,9,13,... points (v = 3n+1 for n even, 3n for n odd, as the OEIS data and the Adams-Watters comment; the title's 6n+1/6n+3 does not match the data). f is an exact-cover count of the pair set by triangles: it branches on the third point k of the least uncovered pair (i,j), which must have k > j, so each system is counted once. The blocks through point 0 form a perfect matching of the other v-1 points; a permutation fixing 0 that maps one matching to another maps the completions bijectively, so a(n) = (v-2)!! times the completions of the fixed blocks {0,x,v-x}; p runs through 1*3*5*... and equals (v-2)!! at each v, and v = 5 mod 6 is skipped. No conjecture.

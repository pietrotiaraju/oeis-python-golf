d2a457d4f69e Definition: a(n) counts the bottom-to-top orders of the 3n stamps of a 3 X n sheet in a flat folded pile (every order counts: a(1)=6). Folding criterion of Lunnon's map-folding algorithm (Lunnon 1971, OEIS link; Koehler's rule for strips as in the merged A000136): an order is a folding iff no two creases on the same edge of the pile interleave; creases between columns c and c+1 lie on an edge fixed by the parity of c, creases between rows 1-2 on a third edge and rows 2-3 on the fourth. Exhaustive depth-first search: stamps are added column by column (middle row first, so the two row creases of a column have offsets d=1 and d=2 and each offset is one edge), each into every gap of the pile (list heights, x+[-p] just above x, as in A000136); a gap is kept iff the new creases interleave with no earlier crease of the same edge ([a%3<1,a//3+p//3&1][d>2] selects exactly those creases; d=0 for a middle stamp compares a stamp with itself and always passes). Restricting a folding to its first stamps leaves a folding, so every folding is generated exactly once. Exact integers, no stored terms; matches the b-file on the 6 terms it reaches and agrees with A001415/A001418 on shared sizes. Rests only on Lunnon's folding criterion.

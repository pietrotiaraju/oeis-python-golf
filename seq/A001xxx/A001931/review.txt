1bc24627ac80 Fixed polycubes = polycubes up to translation (OEIS comment). Redelmeier's algorithm in the cubic lattice, as in A001168 with a third axis: each polycube is generated exactly once from its lowest cell in (z,y,x) order placed at the origin. Cells are coded x+K*y+K*K*z with K=n+2; the page shows that every cell the search touches has coordinates within K-3 of the origin and pairwise differences below K, so the code is injective and 'code>0' is exactly the (z,y,x)-after-origin test. Exhaustive, integer arithmetic only, so correct for every n.

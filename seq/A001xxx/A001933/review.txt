5434bfe871ca Chessboard polyominoes (Mason's OEIS comment): coloured n-ominoes up to translation, rotation and reflection with colours preserved. The program grows one representative per class by adding a neighbour cell (every (n+1)-omino is an n-omino plus a leaf of a spanning tree, and colour-preserving symmetries map neighbours to neighbours). Key = set of the 8 rotated/reflected images of the centred shape (|Q|z - sum Q), each cell tagged +1/2 by its colour Re(z^2) mod 2; the page shows equal keys means Q' = g(Q)+t with every cell keeping its colour, i.e. t even, which is exactly the board-symmetry equivalence. Exhaustive, so correct for every n; complex parts are integers or halves of size <= 2n^2, exact for n < 6e7. Agrees with a(odd n) = 2*A000105(n).

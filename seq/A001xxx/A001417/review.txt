a4cbf9cff418 Definition: a(n) counts the bottom-to-top orders of the 2^n cells of a 2 X ... X 2 n-dimensional map in a flat folded pile (every order counts: a(1)=2, a(2)=8). Cells are 0..2^n-1 with bit k = coordinate k; each dimension has one crease line, so all creases (a, a+2^k) of dimension k lie on one face. Criterion of Lunnon's multi-dimensional map-folding algorithm (Lunnon 1971, OEIS link; Koehler's rule for strips as in the merged A000136): an order is a folding iff no two creases of the same dimension interleave. Exhaustive depth-first search: cells are added in numerical order into every gap of the pile (list heights, x+[-p] just above x, as in A000136); a gap is kept iff each new crease (p-d, p), d = 2^k a bit of p, interleaves with no earlier crease (a, a+d) of that dimension (a&d<p&d selects exactly those). Restricting a folding to its first cells leaves a folding, so every folding is generated exactly once. Exact integers, no stored terms; matches the b-file on the 5 terms it reaches. Rests only on Lunnon's folding criterion.

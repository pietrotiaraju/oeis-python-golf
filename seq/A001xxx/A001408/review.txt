09223aabd1fa Definition: C/(k K^2) = 3*sum a(n) v^(2n) with v=tanh K, the high-temperature series of the specific heat per spin of the simple cubic Ising model (the formula C/(kK^2) = d^2/dK^2 (3 ln cosh K + ln Lambda), Lambda from A001393, gives 3, 33, 564, ... = 3*(1, 11, 188, ...)). The program uses the finite-lattice method of A001393 (Enting's method): for boxes of l x m x k sites with T-5 <= l+m+k <= T and weights (-1)^u C(5,u), u=T-(l+m+k), the weighted sum of ln Z(box) equals ln Z/N per spin modulo v^(2T-5), because a cluster spanning an l x m x k box has an Euler circuit with at least 2(l+m+k-3) edges, and the weights are the collected second differences (they sum the site counts lmk to 1 and the edge counts to 3, so the factors 2^s cosh^E K give exactly ln 2 + 3 ln cosh K). Each d/dK = (1-v^2) d/dv costs one order, so with T=n+4 the weighted sum of d^2/dK^2 ln Z(box), the variance of the box energy, is C/(kK^2) modulo v^(2n+1). That variance is computed exactly from the moment sums S_j = sum over all spin configurations of (1+v)^(E-d)(1-v)^d (2d)^j (d unequal edges), each exactly 2^s times an integer polynomial. Kronecker substitution v -> X = 8^(t^3) modulo X^(2n+1) is a ring homomorphism, every box polynomial is a unit there, and symmetric rounding reads the coefficient of v^(2n) because all coefficients are far below X/4 (torus bound |[v^k] ln Lambda| <= (6N)^k with N=(2n+3)^3). The final //3 is the division in the definition. Exact integer arithmetic throughout: correct for every n (only time-limited).

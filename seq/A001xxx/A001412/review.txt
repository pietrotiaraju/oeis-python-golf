20700824bbe2 Definition: a(n) is the number of n-step self-avoiding walks on the simple cubic lattice starting at the origin. The program is an exhaustive depth-first search over all such walks: f(p,S) extends the walk from p through the unvisited neighbours p+-1, p+-B, p+-B^2 and scores 1 when the walk has n+1 points. Points (x,y,z) are coded x+yB+zB^2 with B=n+1; two points of an n-step walk satisfy |dx|+|dy|+|dz|<=n<B, and dx+B*dy+B^2*dz=0 then forces dx=0, dy=0, dz=0 in turn (each is divisible by B and smaller than B), so the code is one-to-one on every walk and the visited set S is exact. Every walk is generated exactly once with exact integer arithmetic and no truncation, so the output is a(n) for every n (only time-limited).

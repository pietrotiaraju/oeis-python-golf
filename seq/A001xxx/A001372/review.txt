It is Burnside's lemma for S_n acting on maps f:[n]->[n] by conjugation: a(n) = (1/n!) sum over permutations p of #{f : fp = pf}. A map commuting with p is fixed by its value y at one point x of each cycle (f(p^i x) = p^i y), and y may be any point on a cycle whose length divides the length l of x's cycle (p^l y = y); so the count is prod over cycles i of sum of j over cycles j dividing i, which is math.prod(sum(j*(i%j<1)for j in L)for i in L). f(r,*L) sums over permutations of r further points by the length of the cycle through a fixed point ((r-1)! each, //r exact by de Bruijn's form of Burnside).

a78e6f196852 Definition from Somos's comment on the entry: a linear space is disconnected when its points split into two nonempty parts closed under joining lines; the page proves this is the same as the lines with >= 3 points not linking all points (so a(0)=a(1)=1, a(2)=0, and A001200 is its Euler transform, as the entry says). py3.py is A001200's approved generator unchanged (point deletion leaves a linear space, so every class on n points extends a representative on n-1 points; whole orbits under all n! permutations are removed, one representative per class) and counts the representatives whose big lines link all points (closure from one point, n scans). py3-7.py is A001200's approved short-bar program (spaces = sets of collinear triples with the four-triple closure property, minimum bitmask over all relabellings) with the same connectivity test on the triples, which link the same points as the big lines. Exhaustive and exact for every n, no conjecture, no stored terms.

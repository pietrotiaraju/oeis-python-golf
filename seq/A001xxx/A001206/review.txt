8b0ad3ea321e Monotone Boolean functions of k variables are 2^k-bit masks built recursively from pairs of halves a<=b (a&~b==0), and D maps each to its dual, using (a,b)*=(b*,a*); D[0] is the constant 1, so -~D[0] is the next shift. A self-dual monotone function of n variables is (g,g*) with g monotone and g<=g*; writing g=(h,a) with h<=a, the condition g<=g* is h<=a* (the other half, a<=h*, is the same statement). So a(n) = #{(h,a) monotone on n-2 variables: h <= a & a*}, which the sum counts exactly; a(0)=0 (no constant is its own negation) and a(1)=1 (f=x) are printed first. No conjecture.

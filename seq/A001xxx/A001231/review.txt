9fadf36b069d Exhaustive search, for every n, over projective planes of order n (v=n^2+n+1 points, lines of n+1 points, every pair of points on exactly one line) whose first 2n+2 lines are a fixed normal form (lines through 0, lines through 1, one line through 2); every plane can be relabelled into that form (argued on the page), and lines are generated in the order of their two smallest points, so each normalised plane is found exactly once. Isomorphism classes are then separated exactly by a backtracking search for a point bijection preserving collinear triples, which is an isomorphism. No theorem about planes (Bruck-Ryser, uniqueness results) and no stored terms; a(6)=0 is the search running dry. As a check, the search finds 1,1,2,36 normalised planes for n=2..5, exactly v(n+1)(n!)^2 n/|Aut PG(2,n)|.

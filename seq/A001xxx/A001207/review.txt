f3d763de8876 Redelmeier's algorithm on the hexagonal lattice, as in the reviewed A001168: a cell is x+K*y on the skewed grid whose neighbours are c+-1, c+-K, c+-(K+1), and every fixed polyhex with n cells is generated exactly once from its least cell (lowest row, then leftmost). With K=n, two cells sharing a code are at least n+1 steps apart while all cells the search touches are within n steps of each other, so the code is injective and positive exactly on the allowed cells; the touched non-positive codes all lie in [-2K,0] and are pre-seeded into S instead of a d>0 test. No conjecture.

318e9cf84407 Follows the OEIS definition: the sum of the diameters of all unlabeled (free) trees on n nodes. It counts rooted trees of height at most h with Riordan's recurrence R_h = x*exp(sum_k R_{h-1}(x^k)/k), i.e. A000081's recurrence with the level below as weights, one list per level. Trees of diameter at most 2h are those with a vertex of eccentricity at most h; Otter's dissimilarity count (vertex orbits - edge orbits + flipped edges = 1) applied to the Aut-invariant subtree of such vertices gives R_h - SET2(R_{h-1}); trees of diameter 2h+1 are MSET2 of rooted trees of height exactly h joined by the central edge; t = R - SET2(R) (Otter). Then a(n) = sum_d (t - c_d), which a telescoping sum turns into sum_h (2t - V_h) + t - r(n) with V_h = 2r_h(n) - [x^n]R_h R_{h-1} + [n even] r_h(n/2); the page derives each step. Exact integer arithmetic, so it is right for every n; it matches all 30 b-file terms.

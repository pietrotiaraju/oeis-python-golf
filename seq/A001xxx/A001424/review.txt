6148cfb134b6 Burnside over S_n x {identity, transpose}: a(n) = (A001329(n) + A029850(n))/2, the formula in the entry. For each permutation pi (and pi followed by the swap) the program walks the orbits of sigma on the n^2 pairs explicitly (sigma(x,y) = (pi x, pi y) or (pi y, pi x), pairs coded x*n+y); a fixed operation is free on one pair per orbit, with S(m) = #{z: pi^m z = z} = sum of the cycle lengths of pi dividing m choices on an orbit of length m, so |Fix| = product of S(len) over orbits (counted once, at the orbit's minimum). The average over pi is the cycle-by-cycle recursion of A001329 (the cycle through the first free point has length r-j with probability 1/r, building the explicit permutation p and its cycle lengths L); every //r is exact because the average over sigma in S_r of |Fix(g sigma)|, for g commuting with S_r, is the number of S_r-orbits fixed by g. Exact integer arithmetic, no theorem beyond Burnside's lemma; correct for every n.

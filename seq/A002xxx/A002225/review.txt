e7fa6d59f1fa Definition: the least prime p such that each of the first n primes has three cube roots mod p, i.e. p = 1 (mod 3) and each is a nonzero cubic residue (R. Israel's comment). The program scans p = 1 (mod 6) upward from the previous term (a(n) >= a(n-1)) and tests every integer q <= p_n (cubic residues are closed under products, so this is the same as testing the primes) by Euler's criterion for cubes, q^((p-1)/3) = 1 (mod p), valid for prime p = 1 (mod 3) since the cubes are the index-3 subgroup of the cyclic unit group. p_n comes from Wilson's theorem (P = ((m-1)!)^2), and p is certified prime by trial division over d < 4^(number of digits of p), which exceeds sqrt(p) and, from 3 digits on, stays below p (the only smaller candidate that could clash, 13, is rejected earlier because 2^4 = 3 mod 13). Exact integers; correct for every n by Euler's criterion and Wilson's theorem.

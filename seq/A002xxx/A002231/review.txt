c8903cce6b3f Definition: A029932 lists the primes p >= 3 at which the least prime primitive root sets a record; A002231 lists those record roots (p = 2 is left out as in A029932: its only root, 1, is not prime). For each prime k (Wilson's theorem, P = ((k-1)!)^2) the program takes the least g >= 1 that has no divisor in 2..g-1 and satisfies sum(g**e%k<2 for e in range(k)) <= 2: modulo a prime, g^e = 1 exactly at the multiples of the order of g, so among e = 0..k-1 this happens twice iff g has order k-1 and at least three times otherwise (a multiple of k counts k). g = 1 counts k >= 3, so from k = 3 on the root found is prime; for k = 2 it is 1, which does not beat the starting record r = 1. The g search is not capped at k (no theorem bounds the least prime primitive root below k) and ends by Dirichlet's theorem (primes in the class of a primitive root). It prints g when it beats the record r. Exact integers; correct for every n by Wilson's and Dirichlet's theorems.

75fb73a849b3 Follows the OEIS definition literally: k runs over the numbers 1 mod 8 from 9 upward and the program prints the first k that is not a square and is a nonzero quadratic residue of each of the first n odd primes. The primes come from Wilson's theorem (P = ((q-1)!)^2, P%q = 1 exactly for prime q, 0 for composite q including 4), the residue test is Euler's criterion (pow(k,(p-1)/2,p) = 1 exactly for nonzero residues, 0 when p | k), and j tracks ceil(sqrt(k)) so that k is a square iff k = j*j (steps of 8 never skip a square gap once j >= 4; checked against isqrt up to 2e6 as well). The conditions for n contain those for n-1, so resuming the search at the previous term is exact and repeated terms come out. Correct for every n with exact integer arithmetic; only speed limits the bar (18 terms; the search is linear in a(n)).

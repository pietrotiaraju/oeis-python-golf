57c78024e891 Counts positive self-dual threshold functions of n inputs (one per N-class: complementing inputs keeps self-duality, and each N-class of threshold functions has one positive member). Self-dualization: g <-> f = g with x_n = 1 is a bijection onto positive threshold functions f of n-1 inputs with f(x) or f(complement x) = 1 for all x (g with x_n = 0 is the dual of f; conversely g = x_n f + ~x_n f^d is [w.x + (W-2t-1)x_n > W-t-1], threshold, self-dual and monotone; proved on the page). Those f are enumerated as on A002078: Muroga-Toda-Takasu bound on sorted nonnegative integer weights, all cuts, filter all(map(max,f,f[::-1])), then all input permutations, counting distinct truth tables. Exact isqrt bound; correct for every n given that theorem. Bar n = 1..7, hence the review.

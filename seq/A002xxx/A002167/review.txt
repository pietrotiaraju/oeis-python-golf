[x^n] of the free energy per site ln<e^{xH}>/N of H = sum over b.c.c. bonds of sigma_i.sigma_j (Pauli), normalized trace; a(2)=12, a(3)=-24 are 4 bonds/site times one bond's cumulants, C/k = sum a(n)x^n/(n-2)!. Method: finite-lattice method on Z^3 with bonds (+-1,+-1,+-1) (four disjoint bcc copies, unit-translation invariant, same per-site free energy): clusters grouped by bounding box, W = second differences, a(n) = sum W, evaluated by the 3D window sum (-1)^u C(5,u) kappa_n(box) as on A001393. Truncation: by the bridge lemma proved on A002164 (a joint cumulant with a bridge used once vanishes, since partial traces of SU(2)-invariant operators are scalars) a cluster with c cycle bonds and t bridges has weight O(x^{c+2t}); doubling bridges gives a bridgeless multigraph, each of the l+m+k-3 coordinate planes of the bounding box is crossed twice and each bond crosses three planes, so 3(c+2t) >= 2(l+m+k-3) and T = floor(3n/2)+3 is exact for every n. Each box splits into its four parity classes (cumulants add over components); kappa_n of a class is computed exactly over all 2^S spin states with integer moments and the cumulant recursion. No conjecture; exponential cost only.

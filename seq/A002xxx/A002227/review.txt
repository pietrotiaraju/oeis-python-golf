1a65f1a49aa8 Definition: the least prime p such that the first n primes are 7th (A002227), resp. 11th (A002228), power residues mod p. For p not = 1 (mod k) every number is a k-th power, so the entries (data from 631, resp. 331) count only primes p = 1 (mod k); the pages say so. The programs scan p = 1 (mod 2k) upward from the previous term (a(n) >= a(n-1)) and test every integer q <= p_n (k-th power residues are closed under products, so this equals testing the primes) by Euler's criterion q^((p-1)/k) = 1 (mod p), valid for prime p = 1 (mod k) since the k-th powers are the index-k subgroup of the cyclic unit group. p_n comes from Wilson's theorem (P = ((m-1)!)^2), and p is certified prime by trial division over d < 4^(number of digits of p), which exceeds sqrt(p) and, from 3 digits on, stays below p (the only smaller candidate, 15 for k = 7, is composite). Exact integers; correct for every n by Euler's criterion and Wilson's theorem.

a4137dfc79ae a(n) counts the x >= 1 with x and x+1 both prime(n)-smooth. The program uses Lehmer's method (D. H. Lehmer, On a problem of Stormer, Illinois J. Math. 8 (1964) 57-79, Theorems 1 and 2): every such pair gives X = 2x+1 with X^2 - 2qY^2 = 1, q the squarefree part of 2x(x+1) (p-smooth, q != 2), and (X, Y) is among the first max(3, (p+1)/2) solutions of that Pell equation. For every squarefree p-smooth q != 2 the program finds the fundamental solution from the continued fraction of sqrt(2q) (first convergent with h^2 - 2qk^2 = 1), takes the first floor(p/2)+2 >= max(3,(p+1)/2) powers, and counts those whose Y is p-smooth (exact test: P^e mod Y == 0 with P the primorial and e = len(bin(Y)) >= the bit length of Y); the pair's product is qY^2/2, so this is exactly the pair being smooth. Each pair comes from exactly one (q, solution), so the count is exact for every n. Exact integer arithmetic throughout. The bar is 11 terms (p = 31, about 4 s here) because the 12th needs 2047 Pell equations with D up to 1.5e13.

c52a645c0ad1 Definition: the least prime p = 8k-1 (A002223), resp. 8k+1 (A002224), such that the first n primes are quadratic residues mod p. The program scans that class upward from the previous term (a(n) >= a(n-1)); 2 is a residue of every prime p = +-1 (mod 8) (second supplementary law), and each odd prime q <= p_n is tested by quadratic reciprocity, (q/p) = (-p/q) for p = 3 (mod 4) and (p/q) for p = 1 (mod 4), evaluated by Euler's criterion modulo q (pow(-p,q//2,q) resp. pow(p,q//2,q) equal to 1). The primes q come from Wilson's theorem (P = ((m-1)!)^2), and p itself is certified prime by trial division over d < 4^(number of digits of p), which exceeds sqrt(p) and stays below p. Exact integers; correct for every n by quadratic reciprocity, Euler's criterion and Wilson's theorem.

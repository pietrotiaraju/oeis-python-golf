49a8e82ff3c9 Definition: the least prime p such that the first n primes are quintic residues mod p. For p not = 1 (mod 5) every number is a fifth power, so the entry (data from 151 on) counts only primes p = 1 (mod 5); the page says so. The program scans p = 1 (mod 10) upward from the previous term (a(n) >= a(n-1)) and tests every integer q <= p_n (fifth-power residues are closed under products, so this equals testing the primes) by Euler's criterion q^((p-1)/5) = 1 (mod p), valid for prime p = 1 (mod 5) since the fifth powers are the index-5 subgroup of the cyclic unit group. p_n comes from Wilson's theorem (P = ((m-1)!)^2), and p is certified prime by trial division over d < 4^(number of digits of p), which exceeds sqrt(p) and, from 3 digits on, stays below p (the only smaller candidate that could clash, 11, is rejected earlier because 2^2 = 4 mod 11). Exact integers; correct for every n by Euler's criterion and Wilson's theorem.

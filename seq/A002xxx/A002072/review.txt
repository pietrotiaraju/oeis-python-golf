5c23f4e34b80 a(n) is the largest x with x and x+1 both prime(n)-smooth (every k > a(n) has k or k+1 with a prime factor > prime(n)). Same search as A002071: Lehmer's method (D. H. Lehmer, On a problem of Stormer, Illinois J. Math. 8 (1964) 57-79, Theorems 1 and 2) says every such pair gives X = 2x+1 with X^2 - 2qY^2 = 1, q squarefree p-smooth, q != 2, (X, Y) among the first max(3, (p+1)/2) solutions. The program solves every such Pell equation by the continued fraction of sqrt(2q), runs through the first floor(p/2)+2 solutions, tests Y for p-smoothness exactly (P^e mod Y == 0, e = len(bin(Y)) >= bit length of Y; the pair's product is qY^2/2) and keeps the largest x = (X-1)/2. So it finds every smooth pair and its maximum is a(n) for every n. Exact integer arithmetic throughout. The bar is 11 terms (p = 31, about 4 s here) because the 12th needs 2047 Pell equations with D up to 1.5e13.

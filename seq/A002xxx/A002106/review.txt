30abbfa7997a Counts conjugacy classes of transitive subgroups of S_n (the data's convention: a(4)=5 is C4, V4, D4, A4, S4). It enumerates one representative per conjugacy class of subgroups by cyclic extension from the trivial group: every subgroup K != 1 has a maximal subgroup conjugate to an earlier representative H, so a conjugate of K is <H,g> for some g. Skipped g are only h*t^-1*g'*t with h in H and t in the normalizer of H, which give conjugates of groups already formed. Classes are filled by conjugating with an n-cycle and a transposition (which generate S_n), so a subgroup is new exactly when it is not in S. For n>=5 a closure is cut once it exceeds (n-1)! elements: a subgroup of index k<n contains the kernel of S_n -> S_k, a nontrivial normal subgroup, hence A_n; A_n and S_n are added as 2. Transitive = the orbit of 0 has n points. Nothing about the terms is assumed; it matches a(1..7)=1,1,2,5,5,16,7.

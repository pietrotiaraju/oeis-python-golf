65039f297537 Definition: Davenport-Schinzel sequences of degree 4 in the 1970 Roselle-Stanton numbering = no two equal adjacent terms and no subsequence a,b,a,b,a (order 3 today; a(2)=4=abab fixes the reading). The program is an exhaustive DFS over all such sequences with symbols relabeled in order of first appearance (relabeling keeps validity and length), extending by a symbol a != last only if s has no subsequence a,b,a,b for any b != a (exactly the condition that a closes no a,b,a,b,a), tested with the iterator subsequence idiom; it returns the maximum length. The search is finite (length <= 3*C(n,2)+1, shown on the page). No theorem or conjecture beyond the definition, so it is right for every n; bar 6 (n=6 about 42 s here) with timeout 120.

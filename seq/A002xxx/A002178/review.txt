a312f91e5800 Definition: A002176(n)*C(n,1), C(n,k) the closed Newton-Cotes (Cotesian) weights (1/n)*Integral_0^n prod_{j!=k}(t-j)/(k-j) dt, i.e. the Lagrange basis integrated, as in the entry's PARI program. The program computes every C(n,k) exactly: the node polynomial prod_{j!=k}(j+n*y) is packed into one integer in base 2^K, K=n*len(bin(n)), whose digits are its coefficients because they sum to at most (2n)!/n! < (2n)^n < 2^K; the integral is the exact integer sum of (-1)^i*d_i*(n+1)!/(i+1). With Q=(n+1)!*n!, C(n,k)=+-L[k]/Q and A002176(n)=Q/gcd(Q,L[0..n]) (LCM of reduced denominators). It prints L[n-1]/g: the sign bookkeeping makes L[k] equal the true numerator times (-1)^(nk), which is +1 for k=n-1, and C(n,n-1)=C(n,1) by the symmetry t->n-t. No terms are stored; it matches the 21-term b-file and an independent Fraction-based Lagrange computation for n<=40.

c0c50d402539 Definition: A002230 lists the primes at which the least positive primitive root (A001918) sets a record, A002229 the record roots (p = 2 with root 1 first, the entries' convention). For each prime k (Wilson's theorem, P = ((k-1)!)^2) the program takes the least g >= 1 with sum(g**e%k<2 for e in range(k)) <= 2: modulo a prime, g^e = 1 exactly at the multiples of the order of g, so among e = 0..k-1 this happens twice iff g has order k-1 and at least three times otherwise; a primitive root below k always exists, so the search ends. It prints k (A002230) or g (A002229) when g beats the previous record r; composites (P%k = 0) leave g = 1, never a record after k = 2. Exact integers; correct for every n by Wilson's theorem and the existence of primitive roots modulo primes.

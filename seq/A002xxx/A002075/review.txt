4a282a3461d8 Definition: classes of length-n ternary words under rotation and symbol permutation whose words have least period n. Rotations and symbol permutations keep a word's least period, so every class of length-n words has one least period d | n, and cutting to one period is a bijection onto the primitive classes of length d: A002076(n) = sum over d|n of a(d), the relation stated in the entry, so a(n) = A002076(n) - sum of a(d) over proper divisors. A002076(n) is Burnside's lemma over C_n x S_3: (rho^k, sigma) fixes fix(sigma^d)^g words (g = gcd(k,n), d = n/g), which sums over S_3 to 3^g*c(d) + 3[d odd], c(d) = #{sigma: sigma^d = 1} = 6 - (-d^2 mod 6). The divisor subtraction sits in the same sum: for k >= 1, gcd(k,n) == k iff k | n, and k = 0 has gcd n. Exact integer arithmetic, so correct for every n; the 28 terms are the whole b-file.

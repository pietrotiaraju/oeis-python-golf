9be414db274a Exhaustive: a graph with n edges and no isolated vertices has at most 2n vertices, so its classes are those of graphs on 2n labelled points with n edges (pad with isolated points). The edge-set bitmask after a relabelling determines the graph, so the minimum over all (2n)! relabellings is one number per isomorphism class. Exact for every n; only slow.

A regular pentagon with normals 72 degrees apart has offsets h_k whose period-5 spectrum is only 0 and +-1, i.e. h(k+3) = phi*(h(k+2)-h(k+1)) + h(k), so three offsets force the other two; r=0 (concurrent lines) is excluded, each pentagon is seen twice (outward/inward normals) and rotation by a vertex gives the factor n. Edges lie on the chords iff consecutive chords meet inside the polygon, which for arcs of half-widths u, v whose centres differ by 2n on a circle of 10n is |u-v| <= 2n <= u+v <= 8n. All equality tests are sums of at most 16 roots of unity of order 10n, decided in Z/M with zeta -> B = 20n and M = Phi_10n(B) (lcm form valid since B shares every prime of 10n): the kernel ideal has norm M > 16^phi(10n) >= |Norm| of any nonzero such sum, so there are no false zeros.
